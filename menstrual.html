Menstrual Phase 🩸 (Day 1 - 5)

Your cycle has restarted. Time to nurture and listen to your body.

What's Happening? 🧬

  • Estrogen and Progesterone hormones are at their lowest level.
  • The uterus sheds its inner lining, causing your period.
  • Energy levels may feel lower than usual.

Self-Care & Comfort 🧘‍♀️

  • Use a hot water bag or heating pad for cramps.
  • Prioritize 7-8 hours of quality sleep.
  • Do light stretches or gentle walking if you feel up to it.

Nutrition Guide 🍎

  • Eat iron-rich foods like dark leafy greens and lentils.
  • Stay hydrated with warm water or herbal ginger teas.
  • Limit high-sugar and highly salty snacks to avoid bloating.

Hygiene Tips 🧼

  • Change pads or tampons every 4-6 hours.
  • Track your flow variance (Light, Medium, Heavy).
  • Keep yourself fresh and wear comfortable cotton clothing.
