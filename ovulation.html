Ovulation Phase ✨ (Peak Fertility)

An egg is officially released. This is your highest energy phase of the entire month!

What's Happening? 🧬

  • A sharp surge in LH (Luteinizing Hormone) triggers egg release.
  • Estrogen peaks, making you feel confident and communicative.
  • The released egg survives for 12–24 hours in the tracking cycle.

Fertility Tracker 🎯

  • This window presents the peak pregnancy possibility of the cycle.
  • Cervical mucus looks clear, slippery, and stretchy (like raw egg white).
  • You might feel slight twinges of lower abdominal discomfort (Mittelschmerz).

Nutrition & Energy 🍓

  • Include light, hydrating foods like berries, citrus, and raw nuts.
  • High physical endurance makes it ideal for high-intensity training (HIIT).
  • Drink plenty of water to naturally keep your system moving smooth.

Social & Communication 🎙️

  • Confidence, mood levels, and social charm are at their maximum.
  • Excellent cycle days for giving public presentations or handling debates.
  • Libido and natural physical attraction markers are elevated.
