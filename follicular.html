Follicular Phase 🌱 (Post-Period to Pre-Ovulation)

Your body is preparing an egg for release. Your energy and mood are rising!

What's Happening? 🧬

  • Estrogen levels begin to steadily rise.
  • The pituitary gland releases FSH to mature ovarian follicles.
  • Your uterine lining starts building up fresh tissue again.

Fitness & Energy 🏃‍♀️

  • Energy levels are increasing; great time for cardio or strength workouts.
  • Social stamina is higher, making it perfect for planning busy days.
  • Metabolism is stable, helping your overall physical threshold.

Nutrition Guide 🥦

  • Eat fresh, vibrant foods like broccoli, salads, and lean proteins.
  • Incorporate fermented foods like curd/yogurt to process rising estrogen.
  • Keep complex carbs included to support high-stamina periods.

Skin & Mood ✨

  • Skin usually starts looking clearer and more glowing.
  • Brain chemistry supports learning new skills and problem-solving.
  • Great window to try new routines or kickstart creative work.
