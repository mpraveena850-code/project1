Luteal Phase 🌙 (The Winding Down Window)

Progesterone peaks to calm your body. Time to ease into deep rest and comfort routines.

What's Happening? 🧬

  • Progesterone rises to build and maintain the uterine walls.
  • If pregnancy doesn't occur, hormone values will sharp-drop at the end.
  • Metabolic baseline increases slightly, which can increase overall appetite.

PMS & Self Care 🧘‍♀️

  • Mood drops, bloating, breast tenderness, or acne might show up.
  • Pivot from heavy workouts to relaxing yoga, walks, or meditation.
  • Avoid stressful commitments during the latter half of this phase.

Nutrition & Cravings 🍫

  • Eat warm, comforting foods like vegetable soups, oats, and sweet potatoes.
  • Magnesium-rich items (Dark chocolate, bananas) reduce cramping severity.
  • Limit heavy salt intake to protect against water retention bloating.

Sleep & Downtime 🛌

  • Body temperature is higher; keep your room cool for peaceful sleep.
  • Excellent week for reflective solo time, journaling, or organizing.
  • Give yourself permission to slow down and rest as your next cycle nears.
